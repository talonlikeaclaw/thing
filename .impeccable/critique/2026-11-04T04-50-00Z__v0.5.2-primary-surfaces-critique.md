# v0.5.2 critique — the five primary surfaces

Method: dual-agent (A: `CritiqueDesignReview` · B: `CritiqueDetectorEvidence`), both isolated and
neither aware of the other. **Both assessments ran source-level only** — the sub-agent toolchain
mounted no shell and no browser, so neither could sample a pixel. The runtime battery they could
not run was executed in the parent context against the live app: contrast and target rectangles
under a real coarse-pointer emulation, `scrollWidth` at 390, real `Tab` traversal, closed-overlay
`inert`/`pointer-events`, reduced motion under the media override, and
`impeccable detect web/src`. Where a claim below rests on that parent run it says **(measured)**;
where it rests on source it says **(source)**.

Target: the space cover sheet, the record specimen sheet, the type table, the schedule, the command
palette, and the phone shell at 390 × 844.

## 2. Design specificity

**Verdict:** high specificity in voice and micro-structure, medium in composition.

The product's identity is carried by structures a generic tracker would not ship: a type ledger
whose rows end in a mono key and a count in the type's own plural (`12 plants`), an activity margin
that prints the property keys the last write changed, a folio (`PLANT · REV 1`) that discloses the
UUID on demand, and a colophon that names the file the space actually lives in. Those are editorial
decisions that survive the removal of the palette.

The interaction model is conventional — header, tabs, toolbar, table, three status buckets, a ⌘K
sheet, a three-tab bottom bar — and `DESIGN.md` chooses that deliberately. The cost is that the
mono/hairline treatment does all the identity work, and it is a well-populated genre: at a glance
the cover reads "considered developer tool" before it reads "field guide". The world is strongest
where it produces information (ledger, margin, colophon) and weakest where it becomes costume
(`REV 1` with no visible history, an 8px graph of dots, a 12px mono key hint).

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect web/src` returns exactly one finding — `side-tab` on
`components/command-palette.tsx:409`. **False positive**: it is the 2px accent marker the design
system requires at the active row's leading edge, and the same marker is used by the rail and the
view tabs. No overlay was injected (the target needs an authenticated session; the detector ran as
a source scan).

## 3. Overall impression

This is a genuinely authored product with an unusually good sentence system and one systemic
weakness: under compression it trades recognition for density. Two of its most distinctive
gestures — the space map and the file-system colophon — are also its least legible at the smallest
size, and the surface that should teach (the shortcut sheet) is the thinnest.

## 4. What's working

1. **The activity margin's grammar.** `changeSummary()` collapses a write to `created` /
   `completed` / `restored` / `trashed`, names up to three changed keys, counts the rest, and uses
   the owner's own key spelling. It is a report, not decoration.
2. **The empty-state sentence system.** Every empty state names what is missing *and* the next
   action in one sentence, no illustration: `A type is a table. Its properties are columns; its
   records are rows.`, `No links yet — a relation or a mention draws the first line.`, `No type is
   scheduled — open Schema and turn on Schedule for a type.`
3. **Handedness as DOM order.** `ThumbRow` reverses children in the tree, so paint, Tab and
   screen-reader order agree; the sheet close controls move with it.

## 5. Priority issues

### [P1] The space map is an illegible 8px-target scatter on a phone — measured

**What.** On the phone the map is authored in a 390-unit box painted into 342px, so a 12px label
resolves to ~10.5px and labels clip at both edges (`…ep the balcony`, `Water the mons…`,
`Dust the shelves`). The node links measure **113 × 14px** (labelled) and the circles are `r=4`
— far under the 44 × 44 floor, and the band takes ~420px, close to half the screen.
**Why it matters.** It breaks the system's own hard rule, and it is the most atmospheric object in
the product sitting at the size where it cannot be used.
**Fix.** Re-author the compact map at a legible scale, give every node an invisible 44px hit
circle, and anchor labels from the measured text width so none leaves the frame.
**Command:** `adapt`.

### [P2] The schedule's empty state is three negations stacked — source

**What.** `AgendaList` always renders `Overdue`, `Today` and `Next N days`; each prints a heading,
a mono `0` and its sentence. A clear day renders `Nothing is overdue.` / `Nothing is due today.` /
`Nothing is due in the next 7 days.`
**Why it matters.** "Nothing right now" is the schedule's most common state, and it currently reads
as an audit of three failures taking ~120px above the first real row.
**Fix.** When all three buckets are empty, render one band: `Nothing scheduled in the next N days.`
with the horizon switch; otherwise keep the buckets but collapse an empty one to one 13px line
without a heading or count.
**Command:** `clarify`.

### [P2] The activity margin prints keys without a verb — measured

**What.** The margin renders `body`, `created`, `room` as bare strings; the checkpoint-7 audit
called it "a stray key" and it is still shipping.
**Why it matters.** The margin is the space's only narrative; a noun with no verb reads as a
rendering leak.
**Fix.** `changed room · status` for updates, the existing verbs for actions.
**Command:** `clarify`.

### [P2] Three 32px controls are exposed to a coarse pointer on a wide screen — source

**What.** The header `Search` and `Capture` (`min-h-8`) and the record's identity-band `Save`
(`min-h-8`) have no coarse variant; they render at ≥1024px, where a touch laptop or tablet is a
coarse pointer.
**Why it matters.** They are the product's two entry points and its save action.
**Fix.** `pointer-coarse:min-h-11` on all three.
**Command:** `harden`.

### [P3] The new header key chip is 10px — measured at source

**What.** The hover/focus key chip under a table header uses `text-[10px]`, under the system's own
11px floor and the only sub-11px string in the app.
**Fix.** Use the label token.
**Command:** `polish`.

### [P3] The palette re-runs its entrance on every keystroke pause — source

**What.** `.palette-results` is keyed on the trimmed query and animates opacity + 4px on mount, so
every 150ms debounce remounts and fades the list.
**Why it matters.** The one surface built for speed shimmers under a fast typist, and the visual
change stops matching the `aria-busy` change.
**Fix.** Animate the sheet once on open; key the wrapper on `empty`/`active`, not the query.
**Command:** `animate` (reduced, not added).

## 6. Persona red flags

**Alex (keyboard power user).**
`Esc` on a record navigates out of the record (window-level handler) instead of closing the current
thing; with a dirty draft that lands in the dirty-guard prompt. No accelerator reaches a saved view
— the tablist has correct roving `tabIndex` and Arrow/Home/End, but there is no key chord. Opening
a row from the grid costs arrowing to the trailing action column, because `Enter` on the title cell
starts a rename — a defensible choice that is not signposted in the `?` sheet.

**Jordan (first-time collaborator).**
The phone bar's middle destination is `Capture`, which reads as screenshot before it reads as
create, while the masthead says `Capture a record` — the first inconsistency they meet. The save
model is invisible until used: a clean record's only reassurance is the `Saved` pill after
`Move record to trash`, several screens down. `Columns 3` opens a visibility panel, while the
actual create action is a `+` in the trailing header cell — two names, two places, opposite mental
models. The folio (`PLANT · REV 1`) is the third thing on the page and explains itself only through
a chevron.

**Sam (screen reader).** `ViewTabs` renders `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"` with `aria-selected` but
no `role="tabpanel"` and no `aria-controls`, so the selected tab announces control over nothing.

## 7. Minor observations

- The cover ends on two hairline footers: the space colophon, then the global `Markdown on disk is
  the source of truth` — the same thesis twice within 40px.
- `role="tab"`/no `tabpanel` (above) is the only ARIA contract break found.
- The record title has no placeholder: an untitled record is a blank 26px mono line.
- Light-mode `--color-faint` on `--color-inset` measures 4.36:1 — unreachable today because
  `--color-inset` has no consumer, which is itself the finding.
- `backdrop-blur` on the phone bar and the discard overlay contradicts the written "no glass" rule.
- `DESIGN.md` states a 1080px content cap; the shipped column is 1400px.
- `DESIGN.md` quotes a palette placeholder that the shipped sheet does not use.

## 8. Questions to consider

1. If the map disappeared tomorrow, would the cover lose information or atmosphere? It is the only
   element whose cost is measured in phone scroll and whose payoff is a scatter of small dots.
2. Is `REV 1` / `space.yml · America/Toronto · 739e23` a promise to the self-hoster or an inside
   joke? The colophon earns it; a rev badge over an unopened history does not.
3. The palette is the fastest route to everything on desktop and a secondary tab on the device where
   hands are busiest — what would the phone look like if Search were the destination?
