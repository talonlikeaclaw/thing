# v0.5.3 release QA — the stability pass

Run on 2026-11-05 against the live SPA (`http://127.0.0.1:5173`, Vite over the seeded Garden
space) in headless Chromium. Every number below was measured in the page after the app settled;
no screenshot-only claims.

**Release:** `web/package.json` `0.5.3`; the changelog's top entry reads `0.5.3` and no longer
carries `Unreleased` or `upcoming`; the served bundle at `:8080` reports `v0.5.3`.

## Method

- Widths `390 × 844`, `768 × 900`, `1024 × 900`, `1440 × 900`; `prefers-color-scheme` dark and
  light; handedness right and left through the browser-local key.
- Surfaces: cover, type table, board, gallery, calendar, record, schedule, schema, trash; the
  command palette and capture sheet at every width/scheme/handedness; the folded rail at 1024 and
  1440 in both schemes and both handedness modes.
- Overflow is `documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth`.
- Contrast composites each text element's colour over its ancestor background stack and applies
  WCAG 2.1 (4.5:1, 3:1 for large).
- Focus was walked with real `Tab` key events and read from `:focus-visible`, the computed
  outline and the box shadow.
- Reduced motion ran under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and read `document.getAnimations()`
  plus the computed animation/transition properties.

## Contrast

`44` states — nine surfaces plus palette and capture, at 390 and 1440, both schemes: **0 failures
after the fix below**.

One pre-existing failure was measured and fixed: the `DEFAULT` tag inside the selected saved-view
tab, 11 px mono at `--color-faint` over `--color-accent-wash`, measured **4.27:1** in dark on the
Garden accent. It now uses `--color-muted`; the re-run over all four view kinds at 390/1440 in both
schemes is clean.

`transition: none`, and the folded rail's grid transition is `none`.

the sticky rail already satisfy the checkpoint's "reserve the column's width" option, so no
visual change was made.

## Limits

- Headless Chromium in this container has no colour-emoji font, so every emoji renders as a tofu
  box in the screenshots. That is an environment limit, not an app state; emoji rendering was
  verified on the owner's desktop in earlier checkpoints.
- No physical phone or coarse-pointer laptop. Coarse measurements use CDP touch emulation with
  `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` asserted in the same evaluate.
- Hover-only styling is inert in the emulator; those surfaces were verified through keyboard and
  source, not as a hover pass.
