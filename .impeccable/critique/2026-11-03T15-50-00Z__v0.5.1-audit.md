## Surface notes

- **Links panel / graph.** Incoming groups and the `×N` count render when backlinks exist; the graph
  disclosure opens and Local/Space controls are 44 px at a coarse pointer. The empty state is
  truthful only for incoming rows today (finding 1).
- **Search landing.** Palette snippets mark the matched term and the footer count is present, but the
  record route drops the query (finding 2). Clearing/Back behaviour cannot be assessed until the
  query exists.
- **Palette.** Result rows, snippet, arrow wrap, Enter-to-open and Escape work. Phone sheet sits
  above the bottom bar; the field keeps focus and the keyboard path is unchanged.
- **Editor.** `/` opens at a line start and stays literal mid-line; the menu is above the bottom bar;
  toolbar buttons are 44 px at a coarse pointer. The missing heading menu, paste and preview-tap
  behaviour are finding 4.
- **Rename repair.** Not present (finding 5); the current title save is unchanged from 0.5.0.
- **Read-only.** Not exercised with a second account in this pass; the owner-only surfaces were
  driven. The read-only conditionals live in the record panel and view shell and are unchanged by
  v0.5.1.
- **Format.** `FORMAT_VERSION` is `4`. Reads during this audit wrote no object file; the only writes
  were the seed itself.
