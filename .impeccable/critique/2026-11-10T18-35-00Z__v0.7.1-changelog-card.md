# v0.7.1 changelog card — critique and revision

Surface: one release card on `/changelog`, read mode, 390 and 1440, light and dark. The container is
the only change; older releases keep their flat list and folded details.

## What the first version got wrong

| Finding | Why it failed |
|---|---|
| No release masthead | The version pill, title and date competed on one baseline with no featured treatment, so `0.7.1` looked like just another historical entry above a louder `0.7.0` wall. |
| Rows read as settings | Four identical hairline rows, each a title and a 14 px chevron, gave no item count, no sequence and no open-state colour. The card looked empty rather than concise. |
| Weak affordance | The chevron had no disc or hover state and the summary row had no open wash or spine, so the fold was easy to miss despite being the only interaction. |
| Summary was body copy | At 15 px in the same ink as item leads it read as another paragraph, not the release's opening statement. |
| No alignment for opened content | Expanded text started at the card edge, unrelated to the section title it belonged to. |

## Decision

Keep the native `<details>` fold and the factual section titles. Add hierarchy without a second visual
world: one short accent masthead rule, a mono date at the trailing edge, a 16 px opening paragraph,
numbered section rows with a right-aligned change count and a chevron disc, and an accent spine for
opened content — aligned under the title on a wide screen, full card width on a phone. The open row
takes the raised wash and its title the accent, matching the property identity rule rather than
inventing a new one.

## Measured

- Section rows 48 px at 390 (57 px when a title wraps), 44 px minimum on coarse; opened body text
  runs 311 px wide at 390.
- Counts hide below `sm`; the title and chevron keep the row scannable.
- Horizontal overflow 0 at 390 and 1440.
- Dark body text 7.44:1 against the card; summary 16 px, item lead 14/medium, body 14 muted.
- Native summary keyboard toggle, `aria-hidden` chevrons, headings kept as `h3` under the release
  `h2`; closed sections keep their text in the DOM for in-page find.

## Deliberately unchanged

Older releases are not restyled or regrouped: their flat lists and `14 more changes` disclosure still
read as the record of what shipped then. The version sidebar and the page masthead are untouched.
