# v0.5.2 primary-page spec audit

**Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Tree:** checkpoints 1–8 landed (8 uncommitted) · **Spec:** `DESIGN.md` plus
the plan's §4.3 handedness rules · **Method:** live SPA, Chromium emulation; 390 × 844 coarse,
1024 × 768, 1440 × 900; both handedness modes; both schemes for colour claims; DOM measurement
plus screenshots.

Primary pages reviewed: all spaces, space home, type page and its table, record, schedule, palettes
and sheets, phone shell. The unstarted checkpoints 9–10 are treated as scheduled work, not as
regressions; this audit asks whether the shipped surfaces match the written system.

## Result

Three real deviations from the written spec, all in the same family — handedness moving
reading-order content — plus one page title outside the display face. Fixed in this pass.

### 1. Page headers mirrored with handedness (fixed)

- **Spec:** "Reading order never moves; thumb actions do. Page headers, prose, table columns, graph
  nodes and settings rows never mirror." (plan §4.3; `DESIGN.md`'s handedness rules.)
- **Evidence, type page (left-handed):** the whole band reversed, so the `h1` sat at x1256 while
  `New chore` sat at x205. On a phone the title sat at x289 with the actions at the left.
- **Evidence, schedule (left-handed, ≥1024):** `Schedule` rendered after `List`/`Calendar` at the far
  right (x932–996), with the tools before it.
- **Fix:** the header is `[title, actions]` at every width and mode; the action pair keeps its
  canonical order (`Edit type`, then the primary `New <type>` last) so paint, Tab and screen-reader
  order agree. The schedule header is `[title, view switch]` then the tool `ThumbRow`, which still
  moves its own actions. `DESIGN.md`'s type-header line now says the header is reading order, never a
  thumb row.
- **Verified:** left mode — type `h1` x332, `New` x1309; schedule `h1` x292, `List` x480, `Filter`
  x1161. Right mode — identical header/strip order (x332 / x1309 / 292 / 410).

### 2. The view strip moved `New view` to the front in left-handed mode (fixed)

- **Spec:** "a ghost `+ New view` at the end of the row" (`DESIGN.md` View Switcher); saved views are
  navigation and keep their order.
- **Evidence:** left mode rendered `New view` at x292 and the `All` tab at x394.
- **Fix:** removed the handedness branch; `New view`/`Reorder` close the row on every width. The
  compact second row keeps its thumb-edge alignment because it holds actions only.
- **Verified:** left and right mode both read tab x292, `New view` x410.

### 3. The Schedule page title was sans at 26px (fixed)

- **Spec:** the display face sets "the space name, auth and page titles" (`DESIGN.md` Hierarchy);
  page titles use the `--text-display` token, 36px on desktop and 30px on a phone.
- **Evidence:** `h1 { font-family: IBM Plex Sans; font-size: 26px }`.
- **Fix:** `display text-display`, so Schedule now shares the page-title face with the cover sheet
  and the quiet surfaces.
- **Verified:** 36px Maple Mono at the leading edge; the token steps to 30px below `sm`.

## Open items, already scheduled

These are checkpoint 9–10 scope, not landed-spec regressions:

- Table header keys truncate mid-identifier (`last_completed_…`) at 1440; the plan's checkpoint 9
  makes the key secondary/hover-only and stops the truncation.
- The cover sheet keeps a 245px dead band before the graph and draws a 462px canvas for seven
  edge-less nodes while the legend advertises relation and mention; checkpoint 9 compresses the band
  and adds the zero-edge state.
- The schedule's rows and its "today" line print ISO dates while records print `5 Nov 2026`;
  checkpoint 9 applies the shared formatter.
- The shortcuts sheet lists three keys and opens with focus on its Close control; checkpoint 10
  lists the real shortcuts and focuses the dialog.
- Settings keeps a 576px form in a 1400px main and the icon control has no frame; checkpoint 10
  composes it.

Two findings from the earlier post-checkpoint-7 audit were withdrawn after re-measurement and are
not repeated here: the 44 px floor holds under real touch emulation, and the focus rings resolve to
one accent after their colour transition settles.
