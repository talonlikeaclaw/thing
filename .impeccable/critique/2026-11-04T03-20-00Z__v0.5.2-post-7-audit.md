Screens reviewed: all-spaces, space home, type table, record, schedule (list), schema, members,

discarded.

## Strengths

- The cover sheet's masthead, the record's identity band and the type ledger belong to one product
  without labels: mono display, hairline rules, mono annotations.
- The palette reads as a command surface on both pointer types; focus lands in the field; rows,
  groups and footer share one rhythm; the closed overlay is `inert`.
- Focus is visible on all 14 tab stops; reduced motion computes to `0s`; 320 px does not scroll
  horizontally; only the deliberate `border-l-2` marker trips the static detector.
- Contrast is clean outside the wash, in both schemes, on nine of ten routes.
- The coarse floor holds: with a real touch emulation, no control on ten routes is under 44 px except
  the inline `Purge` action (34 × 44) and a 13 px checkbox inside a 44 px row; text fields are 16 px.

## Findings

### P0 — save bar floats over content

- At 1440 × 900: `.record-save-pill` occupies y868–900 while the `Links` block starts at y855 (its
  first line at y884) — an overlap on the heading and its first line. At 390 × 844 on a coarse pointer
  the pill covers the recurrence interval field (element-under-rect check).
- `.record-save-bar` is `position: sticky`, pinned at the viewport bottom at every scroll position;
  `main` reserves 40 px instead of the bar's height.
- Reproduction: open any record longer than the viewport, scroll to any offset, inspect the bottom
  band of the main column.

### P0 — text on the accent wash is under 4.5:1

| Pair | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Accent text on `accent-wash` (active schema name, `scheduled`, count chips, `Preview`) | 4.29:1 | passes |
| Muted key/count on `accent-wash` | 4.11:1 | 4.24:1 |

The wash was admitted to the accent test as a tint, but the pairing used in the ledger and the
segmented control was not part of that gate.

### P1 — cover sheet keeps a dead band and an empty map

- The ledger ends at y268; the `GRAPH` header starts at y513. The 12-column margin (activity)
  stretches the row, leaving 245 px of nothing under the types.
- The graph canvas is 704 × 462 for 7 nodes and **0 edges** (`GET …/graph` returns `edges: []`), yet
  the legend samples `relation` and `mention`. The drawing is a wide ring with no lines.
- Direction: let the ledger column end; cap the drawing height; at zero edges, say so and shrink.

### P1 — table header prints the key permanently

At 1440 on a fine pointer every sortable header is two lines: `Title` over `title`, `Last done` over `last_completed_…`. The
truncated identifier reads as broken text and adds a second baseline to every column. The plan
scoped the key as a hover/secondary line at `xl`.

### P1 — schedule's masthead and dates disagree with the record

- `GARDEN / SCHEDULE` sits left; `LIST | CALENDAR` then the 26 px `Schedule` title sit at the far
  right (x814–996). The title trails its own view switch.
- Rows print `2026-11-03`, `2026-11-05`, `2026-11-17`; the "today" line prints `2026-11-03`. Records
  print `6 Nov 2026`. One date grammar was a checkpoint-3 claim; the agenda was not included.

### P2 — the phone table starves its pinned title

At scroll origin the table lays out `Actions 44 · Title 136 · Room 104 · Due 104 · Repeats 104 ·
Last done 104` in a 342 px scroller, so the pinned title column is 136 px and every sample title
ellipsises; the later columns are reachable only by horizontal scroll. `6 Nov 2026` looked cut at the
scroller edge but is a normal partially visible column, not a truncation bug.

### P2 — smaller composition and copy misses

- **Shortcuts sheet:** three rows (`⌘K`, `?`, `Esc`); opening focuses `Close keyboard shortcuts`, so
  the accent ring marks the dismiss control. Capture (`C`), save, and the editor commands are absent.
- **Activity:** the changed-field line prints `room` / `created` with no verb; four of five rows say
  `created`, so the line carries almost no information.
- **Settings:** at 1440 the form is 576 px wide in a 1400 px main, so the right half is dead; the
  icon row has no control frame. Emoji glyphs render as tofu in this headless environment — verify on hardware.
- **Focus ring:** accent `rgb(200,217,107)` on top-bar controls, near-white `rgb(232,236,228)` on the
  space select, muted `rgb(154,163,152)` on rail links.
- **Changelog:** 403 px measure in a 1440 field.
- **Spaces create form:** four controls on one row; help text only under Timezone; the NAME hint is
  detached at the far right; a large empty field follows one ledger row.
- **Palette:** action secondaries repeat `space` for five rows; only the active row shows a hint.

## Direction

1. **Give the save bar one home.** Reserve its height in the scrollport; on desktop make it a
   rail-aligned status row, on the phone a pill that sits above the bar and never over a control.
2. **Keep the touch floor measured.** 44 px controls and 16 px inputs stay the gate; re-run the same
   script that verified them after every record or sheet change.
3. **Make the wash safe.** Derive it per scheme and assert accent-on-wash and muted-on-wash in
   `accent.test.ts`; wash text becomes ink when the accent cannot carry it.
4. **Let compositions end where their content ends.** The cover sheet's ledger column, the graph
   band and the settings page all stretch past their content and read unfinished.
5. **One header and one date grammar.** Title first with the count as annotation on the agenda and
   the table; formatted dates everywhere except the picker's stored value.
