# v0.7.0 release QA — attachments UX, the library and body images

Run on 2026-11-07 in headless Chromium against the release build (`web/dist` served by
`thing-server`) over a temporary data directory (`/tmp/thing-qa-07`), not the owner's `./data`.
Every number below was measured in the page after the app settled; screenshots were inspected for
composition and are not stored, as in the previous sheets.

**Release:** `web/package.json` and the workspace both read `0.7.0`; `GET /api/health` reports
`0.7.0`; the footer reads `v0.7.0`; the changelog's top entry reads `0.7.0` and carries neither
`Unreleased` nor `upcoming`.

## Fixture

A temporary server over `/tmp/thing-qa-07` with the built SPA and the seeded Garden space, plus:

- A `Photos` property of format `attachments` added to Plant, featured in the record margin and in
  the table's columns.
- Four PNGs at 1200×800, 700×1000, 800×800 and 900×600, a minimal PDF, and one deliberately
  unattached PNG (`orphan-upload.png`), all uploaded through the real upload route.
- `Monstera in the study`: three photos (two images and the PDF), the wide PNG as its 16:9 cover,
  and two body images inserted through the editor's own image action.
- `Fiddle-leaf fig`: two photos, the PDF shared with the Monstera.
- `Balcony herbs`: one photo, then trashed, so the library carries a trashed use.
- `Snake plant`: a cover and no photo set, for the gallery's cover fallback.
- A `Photos` gallery view whose `cover` names the `attachments` property.
- The space health read reported `missing_files: []` after seeding.

## Method

- Widths `390 × 844`, `768 × 900`, `1024 × 900`, `1440 × 900`; `prefers-color-scheme` dark and
  light; coarse pointer by CDP `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled` held open in the same session
  as the measurement, with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` asserted true beside it.
- Surfaces: the record's attachments grid and cover, the library, the lightbox (record set and
  library set), the storage section of space settings, and the body editor in Write mode.
- Overflow is `documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth`.
- Contrast composites each text element's colour over its ancestor background stack (alpha
  composited) and applies WCAG 2.1 (4.5:1, 3:1 for large text). `oklab()` backgrounds are read as
  their sRGB composite; the one white-on-`bg-black/55` pill is a deliberate veil over a picture and
  measures 4.8:1 against the brightest possible backdrop.

## Coarse pointer

With touch emulation held open, the record, library and settings were swept for controls below the
floor. Two real gaps were found and fixed in this checkpoint:

| Surface | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Record: a file row's name link in a photo set | 202 × 21 anchor | 44 px row, name still truncating |
| Library: record chip, trashed chip and the file's Download link | 24 px tall | 44 px tall on a coarse pointer |

After the fix the sweep reports **no control below 44 × 44** on all three surfaces, every visible
input stays at 16 px, and the record's Save bar, the lightbox's Close/Previous/Next, the attachment
tiles' actions, Add files and Paste all meet the floor. The remaining 1 × 1 `<input type="file">`
elements are `sr-only` and opened programmatically, not touch targets.

## Contrast

`main` was walked for every text-bearing leaf on the record, library and settings in both schemes:
**one failure**, the cover pill's 11 px `Cover` label, which is white text on `bg-black/55` over a
picture — a deliberate veil, not the accent wash the measuring pass first assumed. Against the
brightest possible backdrop it composites to 4.8:1; over the dark and mid-tone photos in the fixture
it is 8–13:1. Everything else passes at 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), including the mono counter, the
`Unused` and `In trash` chips, and the storage figures.

## Limits

This machine has no physical phone and no real camera or system clipboard: coarse pointer and the
library/record layouts were exercised under CDP touch emulation only, and the clipboard paste path
was exercised through the same upload route the paste action calls, not by a system clipboard
grant. The v0.4.3 physical-iPhone gate stays open.
