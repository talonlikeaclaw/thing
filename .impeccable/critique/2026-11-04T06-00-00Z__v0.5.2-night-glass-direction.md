# v0.5.2 — The Night Glass direction (2026-11-04)

Owner request: upgrade the design language and layouts to a modern frosted-glass,
circular, floating interface in the manner of iOS, using TREK as the reference, and
determine which surfaces and elements to upgrade. This file records the language, the
evidence it is derived from, and the surface inventory. It is the design source for
the implementation that follows.

## 1. What TREK actually is

Read from the TREK source (`liketrek/TREK`, client), not from screenshots:

- **Ground.** Near-black `#0A0A0C` with a radial screen gradient
  `#17171C → #0C0C0F → #08080A`; light is a warm bone `#F3F2EF` with
  `#FBFAF7 → #E9E7E1`. The gradient is the top-light that makes glass legible.
- **Glass.** `--m-glass: rgba(26,26,31,.6)`, border `rgba(255,255,255,.12)`,
  `backdrop-filter: blur(30px) saturate(1.8)`. Sheets are `rgba(22,22,27,.94)` with
  `blur(40px)`. Inner surfaces are `rgba(255,255,255,.07)`.
- **Shape.** The phone dock is a fixed, inset-16px, 62px-tall `border-radius: 31px`
  glass bar with a soft `0 16px 44px -14px` shadow. A glass top bar is a full pill.
  Nav items are 42px circles; the active item is an inverted circle (`--m-act`).
  The centre action is a 56px circle with its own shadow. Sheets are 26–28px.
- **Controls.** Segmented controls are pill tracks with a sliding inverse thumb;
  icon buttons are circles (38px top bar, 34px sheet close); rows are 16–18px
  rounded translucent fills.
- **Type and motion.** Heavy sans labels, a secondary numeric face, 14px body.
  Sheets enter with a 24px rise over 280ms; popovers 6px/0.97 scale over 220ms.
- **Discipline.** Glass is chrome only. Cards sit on the ground as translucent
  fills, but text and data never sit on a blurred field behind another blur.

## 2. The language for thing — "Night Glass"

thing is not a travel app and must not become one. The transferable language is:
**a lit instrument in a dark room, where the system's chrome floats as glass and the
user's data lies on matte stock beneath it.** It is the field guide seen through the
instrument's glass, not a replacement of the field guide's content model.

**Principles**

1. **Glass is the system's voice, never the data's ground.** Navigation, headers,
   toolbars, sheets, the command palette, toasts, the save state and floating action
   bars may blur. Tables, ledgers, record bodies, forms and property grids stay on
   opaque ground. A table behind a blur is unreadable; a blurred dock over a table
   is useful.
2. **Floating, not full-bleed.** Chrome detaches from the viewport edge: 8–16px
   margins, 22–31px radii, a 1px lit edge and one wide ambient shadow. Content
   scrolls underneath it.
3. **Circular for actions, pill for state, rounded for fields.** Icon actions are
   circles; primary and segmented controls are pills; inputs, rows and cards use a
   12/18/28px radius family. No mixed corners on one control.
4. **One accent, one raised action.** The space accent still marks state. The only
   saturated element in the chrome is the capture circle (accent fill, accent ink).
   Active destinations use an accent wash plus a filled accent glyph, not a second
   colour.
5. **The type voice does not change.** Maple Mono remains the display and data face;
   IBM Plex Sans remains the sentence face. The upgrade is material, shape and
   composition, not a new brand.
6. **Light is the same instrument at dawn.** Bone ground, white glass, the same
   radii, the same floating chrome; the accent darkens for contrast exactly as it
   does today.
7. **Motion is the material settling.** Sheets rise 18px over 240ms on an
   exponential ease; the dock and header do not animate on scroll. Reduced motion
   keeps colour and opacity feedback, drops transforms.

**Token contract**

| Token | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#0a0e0b` | `#f1efe7` |
| inset | `#070a08` | `#e7e4da` |
| surface (matte card) | `#151a16` | `#fbfaf5` |
| raised (hover, inner) | `#1d231e` | `#efede4` |
| glass fill | `rgba(19,25,20,.62)` | `rgba(255,255,255,.62)` |
| glass strong (sheet) | `rgba(21,27,22,.86)` | `rgba(252,251,246,.90)` |
| glass edge | `rgba(255,255,255,.12)` | `rgba(255,255,255,.85)` |
| shadow float | `0 20px 50px -22px rgba(0,0,0,.72)` | `0 20px 44px -24px rgba(40,44,34,.28)` |
| blur / saturate | `24px / 150%` | same |
| radius field / card / sheet | `12 / 18 / 28px` | same |

Contrast is measured on the composite: ink and muted text over glass over the
lightest gradient stop, not over the raw fill. The accent test keeps its current
four checks and gains the glass composite.

### Do not change

- Routes, data reads, permissions, formats, filters, sorts and editor commands.
- The six accent presets and their contrast floor; the shared-accent rule.
- The 44px coarse target and 16px input floors.
- Reading order and handedness behaviour; only the dock's order mirrors.

## 4. Anti-references

A generic SaaS dashboard with cards; glass used as decoration on every panel;
neon-cyberpunk glows; macOS Big Sur pastiche with oversized rounded corners and no
content density; Apple marketing imagery inside a working tool; a travel-app clone
with photographic covers and playful mascots (TREK's content, not its material).

## 5. The protected rules that survive the change

1. One accent per space; stateful only.
2. The markdown store and its readability; nothing here touches files.
3. The keyed ledger: every list entry still leads with its mono key.
4. Data legibility: no blur, gradient or shadow under a table cell or a body line.
5. The 11px label floor and the 4.5:1 contrast floor.
