# Direction contract — the app shell (all signed-in routes)

THESIS: The whole product is a lit instrument at night. Frosted glass belongs to the
system's voice — navigation, toolbars, overlays, save state — while the user's data
sits on matte, opaque ground inside it. The category default this refuses is the
flat hairline dashboard on the one hand and glass-everywhere decoration on the other.

OWN-WORLD: A green-black night ground under a soft top-light gradient; floating
glass panels at 60–66% opacity with 24–30px blur, 1.8 saturation, a 1px lit edge and
a wide soft ambient shadow; every control either a full pill or a 12–18px rounded
surface; 42px circular icon buttons and a 56px accent capture button; Maple Mono for
titles, keys and numbers, IBM Plex Sans for sentences; one accent per space, Lichen
by default, used for state and the single raised action.

STORY: A collaborator opens the app in a dim room and reads one space as a cover, a
record as a specimen sheet, a table as a ledger. The chrome floats and recedes; the
data is still, dense and legible; the active destination is a filled circle and the
primary action is the only saturated circle on screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A floating glass header inset from the top edge with the wordmark,
space switcher, search and capture; a floating glass rail at the left on desktop; the
page's display title below; the space ledger on an opaque card with rounded corners.
On a phone the header is a glass pill; a floating glass dock sits 12px above the home
indicator with three 42px circles and a 56px accent capture circle in the middle.

FORM: Operate. Standard web controls, native routing and permissions unchanged. The
world supplies type, palette, density and one signature move — the floating glass
chrome whose active item is an inverted circular seal. Data surfaces never blur.

RESPONSIVE: One composition from 320 to 1600: the header, rail and dock stay floating
with margins and safe-area insets; the content column stays 1400px max; the dock
clears content and the keyboard; reduced motion keeps colour feedback and drops
entrance transforms.

SEED: none (direction pinned by the brief: frosted glass + circular floating iOS
elements, TREK as the reference implementation).
