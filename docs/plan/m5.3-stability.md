# Milestone 5.3 — the stability pass

**Status: LANDED 2026-11-05 at workspace `0.5.3`.** v0.5.3 is the release between the v0.5
visual work and v0.6.0. It ships no new route, no schema change and no format change: it makes
what v0.5.2 built honest, reachable and quiet, then hands v0.6.0 a stable base. It carries one new
affordance the owner asked for — a desktop rail that folds to its icons — plus a larger space icon
on the cover. All nine checkpoints are implemented; the release is not tagged or deployed.

v0.5.2 landed eleven checkpoints in one day: the Night Glass material, a cover sheet, a specimen
sheet, ledger tables, a command surface and a phone shell. Three audits ran during that push
(`.impeccable/critique/2026-11-04T03-20-00Z__v0.5.2-post-7-audit.md`,
`…04-35-00Z__v0.5.2-checkpoint-9-audit.md`, `…04-50-00Z__v0.5.2-primary-surfaces-critique.md`) and
several of their findings were fixed as they landed. This plan re-audited the shipped result
instead of trusting that list, and it keeps only what is still measurably true.

## 1. Scope

In scope:

- **The two carried fixes.** The space ledger's triple naming of a type, and a titles toggle for
  the space map (§2).
- **The map's label layout.** Measured collisions on both widths; the drawing still lets a name sit
  on top of a node or another name.
- **Touch targets where a finger actually is.** Measured at 390 with touch emulation; five surfaces
  expose sub-44 px targets, one of them because an inline anchor cannot honour `min-h`.
- **The tab contract and the help surface.** `role="tab"` promises a panel that does not exist, and
  the shortcuts button is hidden on exactly the devices that cannot press `?`.
- **The schedule's empty state.** Three negative sentences where one would do.
- **One ledger voice.** The cover now names a type once; the type page and record folio still
  print the stored key beside a name it was minted from.
- **A rail that folds on a desktop.** 208 px of navigation becomes 72 px of icons on request, so a
  reader on a laptop can spend the width on the ledger. One affordance, persisted per reader and
  space, with the phone untouched (checkpoint 3).
- **A cover icon that carries the masthead.** 56 px reads as a favicon beside a display title; the
  tile grows and the summary line keeps its own line.
- **One editing surface in the ledger.** Every format that needs more room than a cell opens the
  same floating editor; the header stops reading as three tones and stops printing labels in the
  space accent.
- **Visual QA and release.** Contact sheet, contrast sampler, overflow and reduced-motion checks,
  changelog and version.

Out of scope, deliberately:

- **No new route, read, format or scheduling change.** The rail's fold is the only new affordance;
  nothing else gains a control.
- **No redesign.** The world, the material and the composition stay as v0.5.2 shipped them. A pass
  that would move a control between surfaces is listed as an open question (§8), not performed.
- **No behavior change beyond what a fix names.** A target that grows, a sentence that merges and a
  label that stops repeating are all this release does.

## 3. How this plan was audited

Runtime probes against the live app (`./data`, seeded Garden), not source reading alone:

- **Viewport sweep** at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 over every route in `router.tsx`; horizontal
  overflow, page height, and every interactive element's rendered box.
- **Touch emulation** through CDP (`setTouchEmulationEnabled` + mobile metrics) so `pointer: coarse`
  resolves, which is the only way `pointer-coarse:` styles can be measured honestly.
- **Contrast sampler** over the cover, the type page, the schedule and a record in both schemes:
  every text node's colour composited over its real ancestor stack. **Result: no failures on those
  four pages in either scheme** — contrast is not an item in this release.
- **Label geometry** on the space map: text and node boxes intersected.
- **Ledger cell editors** at 1440: each overlay format opened on a real row, the row's height before
  and after, the editor's surface class, and the header/checkbox fills read from
  `getComputedStyle`.
- **Source checks** for the contracts a pixel cannot show: `role="tab"` without a panel, the
  shortcut trigger's visibility rules, inline anchors carrying `min-h`, and the rail's fixed grid
  column (`space-layout.tsx:132`).
- **What could not be measured:** a real coarse-pointer laptop at ≥1024 px. The rollups below say
  `measured` for the phone numbers and `source` for the wide coarse case.

## 5. Passes and checkpoints

Each checkpoint is one commit, lands with its own verification, and states its acceptance. Commands
name the Impeccable mode that shapes the pass; `evaluate` runs before and after.

### Checkpoint 1 — The map places names against everything (`layout`, F1, F2)

- Replace the advance-width estimate in `space-graph.tsx` with the painted width: render the label
  once and read `getBBox()` (in an effect keyed to layout), or measure with a shared
  `SVGTextElement` in the same font.
- Treat every drawn node's circle, expanded by a 2 px pad, as an obstacle alongside the existing
  label boxes. Prefer the natural side, then the two lanes, then the far side; if all six collide,
  drop the name (a node without a name beats a name over a node).
- Place names on a word boundary when the title must shorten: `Monstera in…`, never
  `Monstera in the s…`.
- Keep the titles toggle and the phone's whole-map mode from §2; this pass changes only placement.
- **Acceptance:** at 1440 with 15 records, zero label/label and zero label/node intersections; at
  390, every drawn name is on a word boundary; a hidden titles choice still draws all nodes.

### Checkpoint 2 — A finger hits what it aims at (`harden`, F3–F7)

- `local-graph.tsx`: give each node the same invisible 44-unit hit circle `space-graph.tsx` uses,
  authored in the 320-unit compact frame so it clears 44 CSS px at 390.
- Calendar chips on a phone: raise the day cell's record chip to a 44 px row mark, or coalesce a
  day's records into one 44 px row that names the count and opens the day panel.
- Trash and any other standalone inline action: `inline-flex` (or `block w-fit`) before
  `pointer-coarse:min-h-11`, so the minimum has a box to apply to. Grep every
  `pointer-coarse:min-h-` on an `<a>`/`<Link>` and fix the ones whose display is inline.
- Backlink rows and the schema's standalone links: coarse minimum height, same pattern.
- **Acceptance:** a 390 probe with touch emulation reports no interactive element under 44 px
  except the skip link and inputs inside a wrapped label.

### Checkpoint 3 — The rail folds (`adapt`, F14, F16)

The one new affordance in this release, and the only checkpoint that adds state.

**Width.** Expanded 13rem (208 px) stays the default; folded 4.5rem (72 px) fits a 44 px icon in a
14 px inset. The shell's grid column in `space-layout.tsx` becomes
`lg:grid-cols-[var(--rail-w)_minmax(0,1fr)]`, with `--rail-w` set on the shell root by a
`data-rail="wide|folded"` attribute, so the content column gains 136 px in one step without
remounting a page. Folded, the rail keeps its glass card, radius and sticky behaviour; its padding
drops to `p-2`.

**The control.** One button in the rail's own header row, above the switcher: icon
`PanelLeftClose`/`PanelLeftOpen`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` naming the rail, label
`Fold navigation` / `Unfold navigation`, `title` matching. It is a real 44 × 44 target at every
pointer. Bottom-aligned is worse: the rail scrolls, and a control at the end of a scroll is a
control that hides.

**What folds.**

- Space switcher: the native `<select>` stays, but folded it is laid transparent over a 44 px tile
  (`appearance-none opacity-0`, `focus-within` ring on the tile) that draws the current space's
  icon, read from its summary in `list` and falling back to `ThingIcon`'s letter. The picker, the
  keyboard behaviour and the option list stay native. If a target browser proves the invisible
  select unreliable, the fallback is the `Menu` primitive already used by the account menu — not a
  second, differently-behaving switcher.
- `All spaces`, `Overview` and the five utility links: icon centred in a 44 px row, text clipped to
  `sr-only` (never `display:none`, so names survive), the active row keeps the accent wash and its
  icon takes the accent, hover/focus keeps the tooltip. The Schedule overdue count stays
  information: a small accent badge with the number, not a bare dot.
- Types: `ThingIcon` gains a word fallback — an iconless type shows its first letter in the display
  face in the same slot, so two iconless types never render the same tile (F16). The fallback
  applies wherever `ThingIcon` renders, not only the rail; the tooltip carries name and count.
- `Reorder links` is a wide-only affordance: entering Reorder unfolds the rail and does not write
  the preference. Folded, the toggle is not rendered.

**Persistence.** `thing:rail:<userId>:<spaceId>`, `"wide" | "folded"`, default `wide`, malformed or
refused reads fall back to wide; a new `web/src/lib/rail.ts` mirrors `lib/nav-order.ts` in shape and
gets the same storage-fake test. Written on toggle, read on mount, never read on the phone.

**Phone and touch.** Unchanged: the rail is `hidden lg:block`, the dock and the sheet stay as they
are, and folding never appears below `lg`. A coarse pointer at `lg` gets the same 44 px rows in
both states.

**Motion.** Width and label opacity transition over 160 ms; `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets
`transition: none`. Nothing slides in from off-screen.

- **Acceptance:** at 1440 folded, the content column starts 136 px further left, every rail row is
  ≥ 44 px, every link announces its name to a screen reader, the active row is still legible
  without colour alone, reload keeps the choice, Reorder unfolds, and at 390 nothing changed.
- **Files:** `components/space-layout.tsx`, `components/space-nav.tsx`, `components/thing-icon.tsx`,
  `lib/rail.ts` (+ test), `styles/globals.css` (the width token), `DESIGN.md` (the fold and the
  fallback).

### Checkpoint 4 — The ledger edits in one surface (`shape`, F17–F19)

The table is the product's working surface, and its three complaints share one cause: the ledger
has no single answer for "a control that needs more room than a cell".

**Floating editors (F17).** `property-cell.tsx` picks a surface by width alone: compact plus an
overlay format opens the sheet, everything else renders inside the cell. That is why `select`
already floats while `multi_select`, `relation` and `recurrence` grow the row by 91–258 px in
panels drawn inside the cell — two opaque with an accent border, one glass.

- Split the formats by need, not by width: `select`, `multi_select`, `relation` and `recurrence`
  always open an overlay; `text`, `number`, `date`, `datetime`, `url`, `email` and `checkbox` keep
  editing in the cell, where the field is smaller than the cell.
- On a fine pointer the overlay is a `Popover` anchored to the cell button; on compact it stays the
  sheet. Both render the same editor body, so the phone and the desktop cannot drift; the
  `embedded` variant of `SelectInput`, which only exists to fit the sheet, retires with it.
- One shell for the overlay: `glass-strong`, `rounded-card`, a `--color-line-strong` edge (never an
  accent border), width `max(var(--anchor-width), 13rem)`, height `min(22rem, var(--available-height))`
  with the list scrolling inside, `sideOffset={4}`, `collisionPadding={8}` so a bottom row flips
  instead of leaving the viewport.
- While the overlay is open the cell holds a one-line chip (value + caret), so the row stays at
  41 px and the table does not jump under the pointer.
- Commit semantics do not change: single-choice formats commit on pick, `multi_select` and
  multi-`relation` keep their `Apply`/`Cancel` row, `recurrence` keeps `Apply`. Escape cancels and
  focus returns to the cell button in every case.
- The popover is portalled, so the card's `max-h-[70dvh] overflow-auto` no longer clips an editor at
  the bottom edge, and the sticky action column no longer paints over one.
- **Acceptance:** at 1440 every overlay editor leaves the row at 41 px, floats above the table and
  the sticky columns, flips or shifts to stay inside the viewport, and returns focus on Escape; at
  390 the sheet is unchanged.

**One header tone (F18).** The table currently shows three fills — inset band, surface title
header, ground checkbox column — which reads as three materials.

- The header band is one tone for every cell, Title and the checkbox cell included: `--color-raised`
  (a step off the card) rather than `--color-inset`, with the existing bottom rule. A lid, not a
  second surface.
- Every body cell is `--color-surface`, checkbox column included. Pinned columns keep their
  `border-r rule` and the scrolled edge; a column never changes fill to prove it is pinned. The
  selection and hover washes overlay both band and body.
- **Acceptance:** a 1440 screenshot shows two fills in the table (band, body); the checkbox column
  matches the row; the band keeps its tone while the table scrolls sideways.

**Labels in ink (F19).** Property headers print in the space accent (`rgb(150, 50, 95)` on the
seeded pink space) while the Title header prints in ink, so one row speaks in two colours.

- Drop the `accent` prop from the property `HeaderSort`. Labels are `text-muted` at rest and
  `text-ink` when the column is sorted, the same as the title column; the sort arrow carries the
  accent. The format key stays faint mono.
- `DESIGN.md`'s "property names carry the accent in ledgers" becomes: in a ledger the accent marks
  state — the sorted arrow, the active row, the selection — never the column label. Property names
  on the record and the schema page are outside this checkpoint.
- **Acceptance:** no header label renders in the accent, and the sorted column is still identifiable
  without relying on colour.

### Checkpoint 5 — One ledger voice and a masthead icon (`clarify`, F8, F9, F15)

- Apply the cover's rule to the type page masthead: show the key only when
  `type.key !== keyFromName(type.name)`, otherwise let the name stand alone. Reuse `keyFromName`
  rather than a second copy of the rule.
- Decide the record folio's `PLANT · REV 1` under the same rule: the type key is useful there
  because it is the wiki-link spelling; keep it, and say so in `DESIGN.md` so the two surfaces do
  not read as an inconsistency.
- Remove one of the cover's two footers. The space colophon belongs to the space page; the global
  version belongs to the shell.
- Grow the cover's icon tile from 56 px to 72 px (`size-18`), glyph to ~40 px, radius 18 → 22, so
  the tile reads as the space's mark beside the display title rather than a favicon. The tile keeps
  the glass face and the same 16 px gap to the name; the masthead keeps `items-center`, and the
  summary line still starts under the title, not under the tile. Record the size in `DESIGN.md`
  where 56 px is stated today.
- **Acceptance:** the cover ends on one rule; the type page names a default type once; `DESIGN.md`
  states where a key is shown and how large the tile is; at 390 the 72 px tile leaves the title a
  full line and the summary wraps without clipping.

### Checkpoint 6 — The schedule says nothing once (`clarify`, F10)

- When all three buckets are empty, render one band: `Nothing scheduled in the next N days.` with
  the horizon switch.
- Otherwise keep the buckets, but collapse an empty one to a single 13 px line with no heading and
  no mono count.
- **Acceptance:** a clear day is one sentence; a day with one overdue item and nothing else shows
  one full bucket and two one-line notes.

### Checkpoint 7 — The tab contract and the way in (`harden`, F11, F12)

- Give the view tabs a panel: the table container becomes `role="tabpanel"` with `aria-labelledby`
  pointing at the active tab, and each tab gets `aria-controls`; or drop the tab roles for a
  navigation list with `aria-current="page"`, which is closer to what the views are. Pick one and
  state it.
- Keep the shortcuts trigger reachable on a coarse pointer at any width (`pointer-coarse` should
  force the icon-only form, not hide it), and on a phone put it in the space sheet's footer.
- Re-check `Esc` on a record: one press should close the open thing (a menu, a sheet, an editor)
  before it leaves the record, and the dirty guard should be the last resort.
- **Acceptance:** a screen reader announces a tab that controls a named panel, or no tabs at all;
  a phone reaches the shortcut list in two taps; `Esc` closes the topmost open thing.

### Checkpoint 8 — Every read can fail out loud (`harden`)

- Walk each route with the API refusing (`offline` emulation): every page and band shows the
  server's sentence and a retry, and no surface is left on a placeholder.
- The space map, the activity margin and the type table already have this; confirm the schema,
  members, settings, trash, changelog and account pages do too.
- Note in the release notes any write that fails silently today rather than fixing it here (inline
  cell writes are behavior, not a visual pass).
- **Acceptance:** one offline sweep produces no placeholder text and no dead end.

## 8. Open questions for the owner

1. **The map's names.** With the toggle, is a default of "titles off" on a phone the better first
   impression — the whole shape, then names on request? It is one constant to change.
2. **The folio.** Keep `PLANT · REV 1` on the record (it is the link spelling and the revision), or
   fold the key into the history section where REV is opened?
3. **Calendar chips.** A 44 px row per day with a count, or a scrollable strip of chips at 44 px
   each? The row is calmer; the strip keeps the chip look.
4. **The shortcut sheet on a phone.** In the space sheet's footer, or inside the account menu?
5. **Folded rail, types.** Icons only with a tooltip, or icons with a single-figure count (the
   number the wide rail already prints)? One is quieter, the other keeps the count visible.
6. **Folded rail width.** 72 px (4.5rem) keeps a 44 px target and one inset; 64 px is tighter and
   still legal, but the glass card gets cramped. 72 px is the recommendation.
7. **The ledger's header band.** `--color-raised` is the recommendation (a lid over the card). The
   alternative is no band at all: surface everywhere, with a single stronger rule under the header.
   The second is quieter and loses the "header is a different zone" cue on a long table.
8. **Single-choice editors.** `select` and a single `relation` commit the moment an option is
   picked, while `multi_select` needs `Apply`. Keeping that split is faster for one value; making
   every editor confirm is more predictable. This plan keeps the split.
